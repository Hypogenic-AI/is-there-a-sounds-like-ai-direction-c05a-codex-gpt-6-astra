\subsection{Rating rubric and prompt specification}
The judge system instruction defined AI-likeness as how strongly English prose sounds like generic machine writing, explicitly excluding proof of authorship. Coherence anchors were 1: unintelligible; 2: major problems; 3: understandable but disjointed; 4: clear with minor awkwardness; and 5: fluent coherent prose. Coverage measured the fraction of supplied source facts accurately preserved. Unsupported claims measured the fraction of factual claims absent from or contradicting the source. For source-free calibration items, coverage and unsupported values were fixed by the rubric to 1 and 0. The rubric requested sensitivity to repetition, abrupt endings, contradictions, and omission; formality alone did not imply machine authorship, and casual style alone did not merit reward. Texts were to be treated as data rather than instructions. Exact wording and JSON schema are stored in \path{results/judge_rubric.json}.

The standard generation instruction requested one clear 70--100-word announcement paragraph, preservation of all supplied facts, no additional factual claims, and only the paragraph as output. One source record described a library reading workshop, coordinator, time, item count, free attendance, and a deadline two days before the event. Complete records and prompts accompany every generated output. Separate pottery-course records supplied nuisance prompts; no nuisance output was counted among the 1,472 task outputs.

\subsection{Pairing, inference, and selected subsets}
For prompt $i$, let $d_i$ be the treatment-minus-zero score difference after averaging available paired seeds. The primary point estimate is $64^{-1}\sum_i d_i$; the percentile bootstrap resamples prompt indices while preserving their within-prompt pairing. A paired sign flip multiplies each $d_i$ by an independently sampled sign. The Monte Carlo two-sided probability uses an add-one correction over 10,000 draws. Only the two primary external-style tests receive Holm adjustment. The exploratory condition comparisons retain all available paired outputs, including failures.

The matched-quality analysis uses seed 42 pairs with coherence at least 4 in both conditions, absolute coherence difference at most 0.25, and coverage at least 0.95 in both conditions. The instruction mean $-2$ subset has 11 pairs and the base counterpart has 22. These filters use treatment outcomes and do not estimate population preservation. Likewise, length-adjusted regression may remove a mediator of the intervention. We report these checks only descriptively.

\subsection{Deviations from the aspirational handoff}\label{sec:deviations}
The frozen feasible protocol reduced the original target of at least 300 prompts, three seeds, and multiple model families to 64 records, two primary seeds, and paired checkpoints from one family. It used last-token states at three layers, not an exhaustive layer/position study. No SAE analysis, shuffled-label intervention, norm-matched random residualized control, crossed persona/style prompt experiment, dedicated fluency intervention, or full formality-manipulation validation was run. Verbosity contributed to the removed nuisance span but was not a standalone steering condition. We did not run full \textsc{RAID}/\textsc{MAGE} stress tests, an independent likelihood detector, or causal generation interventions on natural \hape continuations. These omissions are not claimed as completed experiments. Base controls beyond signed doses were not run. The full inference pipeline was not rerun twice; repeated smoke batches and a repeated complete numerical analysis were checked.

The project README supplies execution commands. Implementations are \path{src/prepare.py}, \path{src/experiment.py}, \path{src/score.py}, \path{src/analyze.py}, \path{src/audit_analysis.py}, \path{src/supplement.py}, and \path{src/validate.py}. \path{results/corpus.json} records source IDs, excerpts, labels, and hashes; original resource manifests record data revisions. \path{results/config.json} records configuration, and \path{results/tasks.json} plus \path{results/generations.jsonl} preserve full prompts and outputs. Native activations and vectors reside in \path{results/*.npz}; \path{pyproject.toml} and \path{uv.lock} pin dependencies. \path{results/validation.json} records complete scoring, disjoint folds, hook and sampling checks, and identical repeated numerical-analysis hashes. These artifacts support cached reproducibility without claiming deterministic future API responses.

There were 208 successful API requests, including calibration and repeated ratings, with 282,947 prompt tokens and 109,513 completion tokens. Returned usage cost totaled \$0.622884, excluding unmonetized local inference. No ultimately failed calls were present. Equal allocation of mixed-batch costs among items gives approximate per-condition costs; instruction mean $-2$, including its share of re-ratings, cost \$0.0598. Full cached responses omit credentials.
