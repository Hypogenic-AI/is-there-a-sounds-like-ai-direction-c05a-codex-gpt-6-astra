\section{Conclusion}\label{sec:conclusion}
A human--AI residual readout direction changes new writing and judged AI-like style in our controlled Qwen2.5-1.5B experiment. Negative steering lowers judge AI-likeness by 20.04 points, but coherence falls, preservation gates fail, and an external encoder does not corroborate the effect. Strong readout also fails to transfer to base-generator text. We therefore establish partial style control, while leaving a distinct, detector-consistent, content-preserving direction unestablished.

A decisive follow-up should retain signed, random, nuisance, and prompt controls while using diverse naturally grounded tasks, a stronger contemporary paired model family, human ratings, a detector validated on current generators, and a genuine multi-role Assistant Axis estimate. Layer and dose should be selected on separate quality-aware validation data, with preservation tested on a larger untouched set.
