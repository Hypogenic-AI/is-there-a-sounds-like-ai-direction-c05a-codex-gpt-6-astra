\section{Discussion}\label{sec:discussion}
\para{What does the intervention establish?}
Signed additions causally alter generated wording and an external model's perception of AI-like style in this checkpoint and task. Repeated seeds, zero-hook equivalence, and equal-norm random controls strengthen this conclusion beyond readout alone. The evidence does not establish the stronger conjunction of detector consistency, preserved content and coherence, and distinctness. A reduced style score is insufficient when quality declines or another evaluator reverses direction.

The base-generator transfer failure is particularly informative. A direction trained on GPT-4o versus human continuations can capture features associated with instruction-generated text without detecting all machine production. Its availability in the pretrained backbone shows that this readout does not require instruction tuning of the backbone; it does not establish an explicit internal belief about authorship. Near-equal base/instruction AUROC supplies no evidence of amplification under the tested metric.

\para{Distinctness remains unresolved.}
Residualization preserves a smaller judged effect, but the proxy subspace is incomplete and partly content-contaminated. In particular, the assistant proxy is not the published Assistant Axis. Orthogonality to five estimated vectors cannot guarantee independence from persona, formality, domain, length, or fluency. Better vectors, layers, or lower doses could offer more selective control, but these data do not establish such an operating point.

\para{Limitations.}
We study one historical 1.5B model family, 64 templated announcement records, and no human annotators. Base and instruction generation formats differ. The historical encoder transfers poorly, while the judge has substantial re-rating noise and measures both style and quality. Strong additions are large relative to residual norm. The baseline itself often violates the requested length and no-extra-facts instructions. Source matching and lexical audits cannot exclude semantic overlap or pretraining contamination, and \hape filters refusals and incomplete outputs. Common templates constrain the exchangeability assumption; most comparisons are exploratory. API aliases and provider infrastructure remain mutable, and exact local tokens can depend on hardware and software versions. \Secref{sec:deviations} records experiments omitted from the aspirational handoff.

\para{Broader implications.}
The principal measurement risk is treating reduced detectability as better or more human writing. Here, positive steering sharply lowers an encoder score while increasing judged AI-likeness and damaging coverage. Such a result should not be reported as validated detector evasion, evidence of human authorship, or a basis for accusations about provenance. Mechanistic writing interventions need evaluator validation and source-grounded quality checks, particularly when deployed systems could optimize a weak score. Our cached outputs preserve failures rather than selecting fluent successes.
