\section{Related Work}\label{sec:related}
\para{Representation-based detection.}
Quaremba et al.\ use last-token residual features and linear probes to detect machine-generated text across evaluation settings \cite{quaremba2026}. \textsc{SV-Detect} estimates layerwise directions and combines their features for classification \cite{vishnyakov2026}. These methods establish useful readout structure, but their detection results do not establish generation control. We use a single-layer direction in native residual coordinates, enabling a direct intervention rather than treating a multi-layer detector as one physical vector.

\para{Causal steering and persona.}
Contrastive Activation Addition averages paired residual differences and applies signed interventions to generation \cite{rimsky2024}. Kuznetsov et al.\ already steer sparse-autoencoder decoder features associated with complexity, verbosity, formality, and repetition \cite{kuznetsov2025}. Their factual side effects motivate our quality tests; we do not claim the first causal AI-style intervention. The Assistant Axis characterizes and intervenes on assistant persona across many roles \cite{lu2026}. We include an assistant-versus-narrator output-text proxy in the tested model, but this reduced control does not reproduce that multi-role axis. Our contribution is the combined intervention, measurement, and preservation test, including its negative findings.

\para{Matched corpora and distribution shift.}
\hape compares human and model continuations from shared context, revealing grammatical and rhetorical differences across generators and domains \cite{reinhart2025}. \hcthree matches human and ChatGPT answers by question \cite{guo2023}. Such matching controls context or topic, not every factual proposition. \textsc{RAID} and \textsc{MAGE} document difficulties under changes in generators, domains, decoding, and attacks \cite{dugan2024,li2024}. We accordingly separate instruction- and base-generator transfer. Fast-DetectGPT provides a likelihood-based alternative to encoder detection \cite{bao2024}; we did not run it. Instead, we evaluate a historical encoder and an external style judge, and explicitly test their validity under the study's distribution shift.
